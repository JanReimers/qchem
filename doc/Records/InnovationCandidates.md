entry is tentative: a 30-year-old paper describing exactly this could turn up at any time.  So an entry says what the
technique is, where it lives, what evidence we have, and the prior art we already KNOW to check.  It never claims
novelty.

The prior-art checks are open work and live in `doc/OpenWork.md` (the "prior-art check" row), not here.  Their
verdicts come back to this file.  User's ranking: **3 is perhaps the most innovative and consequential.**

## How an entry changes

- A prior-art check finds the technique published: the entry stays, marked **PRIOR ART**, with the citation and what
  differs, if anything.  Never delete one silently.
- A check finds nothing: it says what was searched and when.  That is still not a claim of novelty.
- A new candidate: the user adds it, or proposes it to the controller.
